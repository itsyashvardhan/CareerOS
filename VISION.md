# CareerOS Vision

## The Unfair Advantage

Every job seeker faces the same asymmetry: recruiters know everything (who applied, what they're worth, who else is in process), candidates know almost nothing.

CareerOS exists to close that gap for one specific person: a fresher in India or ASEAN — smart, capable, early in their career — who has never had a mentor at a FAANG or a referral from a classmate at McKinsey. They're competing on merit alone, against people who got coaching, got referrals, got inside tracks.

CareerOS is that inside track.

---

## What We're Building Toward

### The Operating System, Not a Tool

CareerOS should feel like having a senior career advisor sitting next to you — one who has read every job description, knows every company's interview style, remembers everything you've ever told them, and works 24/7.

Not a tracker. Not a template generator. An **operating system for your career**.

---

## The Six Pillars

### 1. Discovery Before Everyone Else
Jobs die within 72 hours of posting. The best candidates apply in the first 12.  
CareerOS hits ATS APIs directly — before LinkedIn indexes, before aggregators pick up, before the role gets flooded.  
**Dream state**: a live stream of opportunities, sorted by match score, appearing the moment they're posted.

### 2. Intelligent Match, Not Keyword Soup
"Does my CV match this role?" is the question every candidate asks and no tool answers honestly.  
CareerOS uses your actual CV to score every opportunity — not keyword matching, but semantic fit — so you spend 5 minutes on the 90% match, not 30 minutes on the 40% match.  
**Dream state**: a daily digest of your top 5 matches, with exactly what to change in your CV for each.

### 3. Company Intel at Your Fingertips
Interviewers expect you to know the company. Most candidates don't beyond the homepage.  
CareerOS researches every company you interact with: funding, tech stack, culture, red flags, what they actually look for in interviews.  
**Dream state**: before every application, a one-page brief — what they do, who they are, how to talk to them.

### 4. Prepared, Not Just Applied
Getting to interview is the start. Winning the interview is the game.  
CareerOS generates role-specific interview questions with model answers based on your actual CV — not generic "tell me about yourself" prep, but "this company asked about X last quarter and your resume has a gap there."  
**Dream state**: a practice mode that scores your answers and tells you exactly what to fix.

### 5. The Follow-through System
Most offers go to candidates who followed up. Most candidates never do.  
CareerOS tracks every application with a follow-up date, generates the exact email to send on day 5, and reminds you when it's time.  
**Dream state**: a complete outreach sequence — application confirmation, day 5 follow-up, day 14 check-in, referral ask — all drafted, all in your voice.

### 6. One Profile, Every Application
The most painful part of job searching is filling out the same form 200 times.  
CareerOS stores your complete profile and pre-fills every ATS form with one click.  
**Dream state**: press Apply, confirm the data, submit — 30 seconds per application.

## The User

**Yashvardhan, 21, Bangalore.**  
Computer Science grad. 7.8 CGPA. No internships at big names. Knows Python, SQL, some React.  
She's applying for Customer Success, Solutions Engineer, and AI Trainer roles across India and Singapore.  
She has 3 months before she needs income. She has no network. She has CareerOS.

Every feature we build should answer: **does this help Aarohi get an offer?**

---

## Roadmap Principles

1. **Speed over polish** — a working feature shipped today beats a perfect one next month  
2. **Real data over mocks** — every API call should use live data, never fake it  
3. **Local-first** — user data never leaves their machine unless they explicitly send it  
4. **One-file simplicity** — the entire app is one HTML file; this is a feature, not a limitation  
5. **Offline capable** — cached data, localStorage persistence, works on a train

They applied to 40 roles. They had a match score ≥70 on 15 of them. They prepped for 3 interviews using the prep panel. They followed up on 8 applications. One of those follow-ups got a response.  
That response became an offer.

That's the dream. That's CareerOS.
